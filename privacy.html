Privacy Policy

What this site collects

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, forms, or third-party scripts, and it does not collect personal information from visitors. The site is hosted by Hostinger and delivered through Cloudflare. Both may process standard connection data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) and keep server logs for security, performance, and troubleshooting. I do not have access to any of this beyond what those providers make available, and I do not use it to identify visitors.

If you choose a light or dark display mode, that preference is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent to me or anyone else.

If you email me, I will use your message only to reply to you and will not sell or share it.
