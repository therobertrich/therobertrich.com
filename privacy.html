Privacy Policy

What this site collects

This site does not use advertising or forms, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from visitors. The site is hosted by Hostinger and delivered through Cloudflare. Both may process standard connection data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) and keep server logs for security, performance, and troubleshooting. I do not have access to any of this beyond what those providers make available, and I do not use it to identify visitors.

If you choose a light or dark display mode, that preference is saved only in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent to me or anyone else.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages are read. The tag loads on every page. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser (named _ga and _ga_ followed by the property ID) to tell visitors apart. It receives the page addresses you view, the referring page, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interaction events such as clicks. I review aggregate reports and do not use this data to identify individual visitors. Google processes the data under its own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

To opt out, block Google Analytics with your browser or a content blocker, or delete the _ga cookies. Google also offers a browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

If you email me, I will use your message only to reply to you and will not sell or share it.
