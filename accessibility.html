Accessibility Statement

Accessibility statement

This site aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, which also meets the technical requirements of Section 508. It uses semantic HTML, a skip link, keyboard-visible focus, sufficient color contrast, text that resizes and reflows, a light and dark theme toggle (dark by default), and respects reduced-motion and forced-colors settings.

Links to other websites open a confirmation dialog that names the destination before you leave. Choose OK to continue or Cancel to stay on this page. Links within therobertrich.com and its subdomains open directly.

Last reviewed: October 2026. If you find a barrier or need this information in another format, email contact@therobertrich.com and I will respond promptly.
